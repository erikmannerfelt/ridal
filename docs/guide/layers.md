# Layers

:::{admonition} Not written yet

Defining the layers a project is picked into. Covers reducers (how several picks by one person at one position collapse to one value, and why `shallowest` is the default), overhangs (why a layer must be a function of trace by default, when to allow them, and why a layer that allows them is exported as its picked vertices rather than at even spacing), duplicate warnings, and exclusivity groups: layers that cannot both hold a value at one position, what the viewer refuses while drawing, and why conflicting values become NaN.
